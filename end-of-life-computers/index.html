End-of-life computers, a second life in schools

These four videos were filmed in a working AI and robotics lab that ZeroAI Technologies built for a school in Ludhiana, Punjab, India. Its 30 student workstations run on end-of-life Dell Wyse 3040 thin clients: machines that would otherwise have been scrapped.

On that hardware, students learn electronics and Arduino simulation, block coding, Python with real machine-learning libraries, and 3D design, on ZeroAI's own offline operating system built on Debian. The robots, drones and smart-home projects in the videos were built by the students.

Our request: 20,000 end-of-life computers for schools in Africa and India.

The governments and schools we work with cover shipping from your site. We take the machines off your hands, so they are reused in classrooms instead of becoming e-waste.
