ZeroAI Studio: the connected STEM classroom

Six applications for electronics, coding, 3D design and AI, from grade 1 to grade 12, in the browser. The teacher casts their screen live to every student, pauses the class with one click, and sets quizzes and competitions. Everything shown was recorded on the online edition, with a teacher and three students signed in at once.
